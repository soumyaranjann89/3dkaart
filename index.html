Who We Are
Bhubaneswar's
3D Pioneer

3DKaart was founded in Bhubaneswar with one mission — to bring world-class additive manufacturing to Odisha. We bridge the gap between imagination and reality for startups, engineers, artists, educators, and businesses across the region.

Our studio at Tamando is equipped with professional-grade FDM and resin printers, operated by experienced designers and engineers passionate about precision — one layer at a time.
